Size: MX fixed it, PA caught it

Over the winter MX ran 10% of its reprints as size errors (484), peaking at 15–16% in November and March; since April it has fallen to 4% and July was almost zero. PA went the other way — 3% in winter, then 8–12% in April–May (over 100 in eight weeks, twenty in one week) before dropping back under 5% from mid-June. CA sits at 6–9% all year. Whatever changed at MX in the spring and at PA in June is the thing to write down.

The glaring ones per plant

PA: incomplete prints at 11% of reprints (305) against 2–3% elsewhere, over-production at 3.4% (95) against under 0.5%, and the two press defects — head strikes and encoder banding — at 11% combined. MX: drop-out banding 9.3% (266), stamping 3.5%, over-spray 2.3% and wrong file 3.2%, each several times the other plants. CA: ink spots 12.9% (185) and location changes 5.7% (82). Everywhere: "other" is 15–18% of all reprints and "graphic not received" 6–8%.

What I make of it

Size

The 3.6-to-1 too-small skew says compensation tables are systematically short on shrink rather than noisy, and the by-press table says it's the table, not the printer. The two things worth knowing are what MX did between March and April (484 winter size reprints down to a few dozen a month) and what PA did in mid-June (from 20 a week to 1–2). If either was a compensation-table update, the other plants should copy it; backlit needs its own correction everywhere, since it is only ever too small.

The rest

PA's 305 incomplete prints and 95 over-productions are the largest single avoidable block in the company — over-production in particular is free money. MX's drop-out banding (266) is the biggest press-defect line at any plant and is the 325-2 story from the earlier report. CA's ink spots (185) are its whole quality problem. And the 1,205 "other" entries across the three plants are a fifth of everything; a reason picker that forces a code would make every one of these tables sharper.
