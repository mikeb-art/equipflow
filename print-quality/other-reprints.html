What to attack, top down

1 · PA: orders moved to PA that never generated in the PA print queue

51 reports, 192 pieces, June 25 – July 1, all filed by management with notes like “export issue, did not generate in PA print que when moved”. Silverflag and Stretch orders transferred from MX/CA. These were never defects — a reprint was the only way to get the job printed — but they sit in PA's June reprint count. Nothing since July 1, so it looks fixed; worth confirming with Manny that the location-transfer bug is closed and that a transfer failure can be logged as something other than a reprint.

2 · PA 325‑1 Stretch: “blue lines, paper wrinkled”

14 reports June 11 → September 21, one piece each, every one on Durst 325‑1 PA with Stretch Fabric, filed by QC. Small in count but it is the one recurring defect in the bucket that has no code and no owner. CA had the same thing on its 325‑1 on June 2 (“white line from paper”, 8 reports / 16 pieces, all Stretch). Transfer paper creasing on the press: check paper path tension and the unwind/rewind on 325‑1 PA, and whether the paper rolls it's fed are stored differently from 325‑2's. Add a reason code so it is countable.

3 · The reason list itself

86% of “other” notes are a standard reason typed by hand, and the Mexico notes are in Spanish. Keep the bilingual list visible at every step, never fall back to a bare “other”, and make “other” require a note of at least a few words. That alone takes MX “unclassified” from 18% to about 1% going forward.

4 · Reprint used as a status tool

Since July, a quarter of what is left in “other” is people fixing the app rather than reporting a defect: “entered finished but still needs sewn”, “sew button doesn't show”, “no RP needed”, “test”. PSU needs a send‑back‑to‑sewing / undo‑status action so these never become reprints. Also: “over production — no RP needed” should be blocked from creating a reprint at all.

5 · MX: wrong file / proof sheet

The largest true residual at MX since July (13 of 22 “other” reports) is “impresion de archivo incorrecto” and “error en proof sheet” — the code exists, and it points at prepress rather than the floor. Worth a look at how MX pulls files for reprints.

Not worth chasing

The 399 blank / N/A reports: 230 of them requested zero pieces (status edits), they ended July 10, and their roles (sewers, warehouse, cutters) match location changes and finishing fixes rather than print defects.
