MX is up, CA is down, PA is flat on reports but way down on pieces

MX went from 10.4 to 12.7 reports per 100 lines, driven by sublimation defects, graphic-not-received and stamping. CA dropped from 8.3 to 6.0. PA's report rate barely moved, but pieces per 100 lines fell from 42 to 26 — the spring's big multi-piece reprints (size, June's print-queue issue) are gone.

The picker is clean now

Since August every reprint carries a proper code — no blanks, no Spanish labels, and "other" is 1–6% at each plant. These numbers need no re-filing.

August → September: what moved

The twelve biggest reasons at each plant, August against September (September is 26 days, so a flat count is a slight rise). Red is up by 8 or more, green is down by 8 or more.

PA: graphic not received 8 → 88

The September jump the Durst-log check already looked at: about 40% never printed, the rest printed complete and were lost after the calender. Meanwhile ink spots fell 114 → 44, incomplete prints 107 → 22 and sublimation defects 43 → 2, so PA's printer and Monti got better in September and its scan discipline got worse.

MX: sublimation defects 21 → 77, graphic not received 50 → 86

Tiger stripes fell 68 → 42 but sublimation defects more than tripled, mostly on the 325‑1's Stretch. Worth checking whether the two are the same Monti problem being coded differently since mid-September.

CA: steady

171 → 168 with no reason moving more than ten; ink spots halved, tiger stripes on backlit rose 13 → 21.
