DOC White Point Report

What the data shows

  1. CA and MX whites are steady. The plant-wide monthly shift stayed within about ±0.15 L* and ±0.25 b* at CA, and within ±0.3 L* and ±0.15 b* at MX, for the whole period. The typical week-to-week change in b* is 0.3 at CA and 0.2 at MX, and the median material shows essentially no drift (CA −0.07, MX +0.04 b* per year). MX whites have read slightly darker since May 2026, about 0.15–0.25 L*.
  2. PA's whites moved together after each recalibration. In Jul–Sep 2025 PA whites read about 0.6–1.4 L* lighter and 1.2–1.7 b* bluer than their medians. After the Nov 2025 recalibration they read about 1.5 L* darker and 0.7–1.2 b* yellower. Since the Mar 2026 recalibration they have stayed within about ±0.3 L* and ±0.3 b*, apart from Sep 2026 (−0.8 b*), a month when most PA DOCs could not be read.
  3. Some PA DOCs read far too bright and are left out. DOCs from Sep 17–24, 2025 and Dec 24, 2025 read the white 5–14 L* above reference across the whole chart, on every printer. 19 PA DOCs are flagged for this, along with 4 backlit DOCs read in a different mode than their references and 3 MX SilverBack DOCs measured against the CA reference.
  4. Single-material steps look like media or lot changes. CA P5-350 SuperFlat went from −1.84 to −5.65 b* between Jul 24 and Jul 25, 2025, and has held there. MX P5-350 SuperFlat, PVC and Durst 325-2 Stretch rose more than 4 b* between Jul 2 and Aug 5, 2025 on the same reference. PA P5-350 SuperFlat went from about −3 to +2.5 b* when it was re-referenced on Mar 25, 2026. CA Tent Direct read about 2.6 L* darker for three weeks in July 2026.
  5. Real slow drift is limited to a few materials. MX P5-350 SuperFlat is getting bluer by about 1.1 b* per year across 66 DOCs that move only about 0.1 b* week to week. MX PVC (−0.7 b*/yr), CA P5-Tex SilverBack (−0.7 b*/yr, +1.0 L*/yr) and CA P5-350 SuperFlat (−0.5 b*/yr) are drifting the same way. With every weekly DOC included, the slow yellowing on P5-Tex fabrics seen in the monthly sample does not hold up.
  6. The whites furthest from their references today are at PA. PA Durst 325-1 and 325-2 Stretch (ΔE00 2.28 and 2.08) read about 1.5 L* lighter and 2.2–2.4 b* bluer than their Nov 2025 references, a leftover of the recalibration shifts. PA P5-Tex Tent (2.18) and P5-Tex Mesh (2.07) are next. Outside PA, MX P5-Tex-2 SilverBack (1.95) reads about 2.9 L* darker than its reference.

How this was made

Every Barbieri DOC PDF linked in the three plants' upload sheets from July 2025 to October 2026 was read for patch 1E2, the 0/0/0/0 paper patch. Where the PDF labels 1E2 it was used directly; otherwise 1E2 was found by matching its reference values from a labelled DOC on the same reference; failing that, the lightest near-neutral patch was used. Every patch value taken was checked by recalculating ΔE00 from its reference and measured L*a*b*. Each DOC's printer and media come from its reference file name, not the sheet column, because several columns hold other materials. DOCs that read abnormally (white 4+ L* above its reference, backlit read in the wrong mode, or measured against another plant's reference) are shown as hollow points and left out of every trend. Drift per year uses only the steady period after the last step at each plant. Printer and media setups last used before Oct 2025 (retired media or references) are left out.
