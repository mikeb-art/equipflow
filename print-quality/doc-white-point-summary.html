DOC White Point Summary

Barbieri DOC paper white (patch 1E2) · CA, MX, PA · Jul 2025 – Oct 2026 · 1,559 DOCs across 52 printer and media setups

Bottom line: Color measurement at CA and MX has been stable for 15 months. PA’s readings shifted plant-wide twice, each time after its Barbieri was recalibrated, and had two bad stretches that were left out. PA’s Stretch references now need rebuilding, and a few fixes to how DOCs are filed would make the data cleaner.

Key findings

  1. CA MX Stable. Plant-wide, whites stayed within about ±0.3 L* and ±0.25 b* all period, with no real drift for most materials.
  2. PA Measurement shifts after recalibration. All PA whites moved together after the Nov 2025 recalibration (about 1.5 L* darker, 1 b* yellower) and again after Mar 2026. PA SuperFlat, which carries the most optical brightener, swung furthest (b* about −14, then −3, then −7), pointing at how the instrument reads brighteners. 19 DOCs from Sep 17–24 and Dec 24, 2025 read 5–14 L* too bright and were excluded. PA has been steady since March.
  3. Most single-material jumps are media or lot changes, not printer drift. Examples: MX SuperFlat, PVC and 325-2 Stretch (Aug 2025), CA Tent Direct (3 weeks in Jul 2026).
  4. Only one clear slow drift: MX P5-350 SuperFlat is getting bluer by about 1.1 b* per year.
  5. Furthest from reference today: PA Durst 325-1 and 325-2 Stretch (ΔE00 2.3 and 2.1), a leftover from the recalibration shifts.

Plant-wide white b* shift by month (median across each plant’s materials; higher = yellower)

Recommended actions

  • PA: rebuild the 325-1 and 325-2 Stretch references; confirm the backlit measurement mode and rebuild the Backlit Film reference; export DOC PDFs straight from the Barbieri software (44 recent DOCs were saved as images and can’t be read).
  • All plants: add a roll/lot number to the DOC form so media changes can be confirmed, and fix the mislabeled DOC sheet columns.
  • MX: check the SuperFlat lot history behind its slow drift.
