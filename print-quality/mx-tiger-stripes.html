No season

MX had the widest humidity swing of any plant, 43% in January to 78% in August, and tiger stripes ran 5–10% of reprints all winter and 1–12% all summer with no relationship to it in either direction.

Two machines, one shift, one material

On stretch fabric — the material that carries MX's tiger stripes — Monti 3 and Monti 4 stripe about 2 in every 100 lines, and Monti 3 on nights runs 2.5× its day rate. Monti 1 runs Silverflag and tent and barely stripes at all. The stretch lines scanned as the 858 or Klieverik 1 (which don't run stretch) all fall in one window, May 22 – Jun 18, across twenty scanners — a picker or list problem for a month, not a person — and June was the plant's cleanest month.

Best theory and what to do

Theory

MX's tiger stripes are set by the operator at Monti 3 and Monti 4, on stretch fabric, and mostly at night. Material is stable roll to roll and humidity swung 35 points with no effect. On the same two machines and the same material, the steadiest operator runs 0.4 per 100 and two day operators ran nearly 200 lines clean, while the two highest night operators run 6 per 100 — a fifteen-fold spread with nothing else changed. The machine matters (both are set up loosely enough that a careless setup shows), but the hands matter more: felt tension, take-up tension, nip pressure and alignment being set differently by different people, not the air in the room.

Suggestions

1. Take the settings and routine of the operator who runs 0.4 per 100 on Monti 3 and 4 and make them the written standard for both machines — felt tension both sides, take-up tension, pressure, speed, temperature, and how the paper and fabric are led in. 2. Start-of-shift check against that sheet, with the night crew trained on it first. 3. One controlled roll on Monti 3: the steady operator and a high-rate night operator, same roll, same day — that is the whole argument in one afternoon. 4. Find out what happened to the machine picker between May 22 and Jun 18 and lock it so the 858 and Klieverik can't be chosen for stretch; keep recording the sublimator on reprints. Humidity can wait until the operators match.
