Tiger stripes, humidity and the calenders

One year of tiger-stripe reprints per plant (NetSuite Nov–Mar, PSU Apr–Sep) against %RH at each plant's Monti sensor, with the maintenance timeline, the calender and operator comparisons, and a best theory and next steps for each plant. Operator names are not shown.

Plants

Pennsylvania

Winter spike of 206 reprints at 19–31% RH lined up with the Monti 2 take-up and Monti 1 felt-tracking repairs; fell within two weeks of the March service. Dry air and a bad calender can't be told apart this year.

Mexico

No humidity pattern across a 35-point swing. On stretch fabric, Monti 3 and 4 run ~2 per 100 with a fifteen-fold spread between operators and a night-shift penalty — an operator-setup problem, not the room.

California

Small numbers and flat across seasons; half the year's stripes fall in two burst weeks on one press each — a setup that was wrong for a few days, not weather.
